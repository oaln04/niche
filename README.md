# 🔍 niche.

a personality quiz that figures out exactly what kind of person you are on the internet.

not your star sign. not your mbti. the real one.

## live

**[niche.oaln04.xyz](https://niche.oaln04.xyz)**

## what it does

6 questions. no fluff. you get placed into one of 6 hyper-specific internet archetypes that actually exist — the kind you clock immediately when you see someone's profile.

- **the letterboxd larper** — strong opinions on films they half-watched
- **the egirl** — pink room, discord open, val rank higher than gpa
- **the soft gym bro** — PR'd his deadlift, cried to frank ocean on the drive home
- **khaleeji in london** — harrods bag, summer only, voice notes in gulf dialect
- **the looksmaxxer** — mewing since 2022, jaw unchanged, still mewing
- **that ecomm guy** — scaling. 14 tabs open. made $340 total.
